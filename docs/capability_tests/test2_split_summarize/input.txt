The Jetson Nano is a small single-board computer made by NVIDIA. It has a quad-core ARM processor and a 128-core Maxwell GPU. Despite its age, it remains popular for embedded machine learning projects because of its low cost and compact size.

Bubble sort is one of the simplest sorting algorithms taught in computer science courses. It repeatedly steps through a list, compares adjacent elements, and swaps them if they are in the wrong order. Although easy to understand, it is inefficient for large datasets compared to algorithms like quicksort or mergesort.

A hotspot is a feature on a mobile phone that lets other devices connect to the internet through the phone's own cellular or WiFi connection. Hotspots are convenient for providing temporary internet access to a laptop or another device when no other network is available nearby.
